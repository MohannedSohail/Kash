## 2. Core Value Propositions

1. **Precision in Your Pocket**: Institutional-grade exchange rates directly from FreeCurrencyAPI, without hidden spreads or inflated margins.
2. **Built for the Levant & the World**: Native first-class support for **USD, EUR, NIS (ILS), and JOD**, incorporating official monetary pegs (Central Bank of Jordan USD peg: $1 \text{ USD} = 0.7090 \text{ JOD}$).
3. **Always Available (Offline-First)**: Intelligent local caching ensures you can convert currencies instantly during flights, at checkpoints, or in remote cafes without cellular connectivity.
4. **Zero-Ad Respect**: No intrusive banner ads, no popups, no data tracking, and no required accounts. Just instant financial clarity.
